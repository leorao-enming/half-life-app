# Half-Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Half-Life is a wellness app that provides estimates about caffeine intake and sleep timing. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Data stored on your device

Half-Life stores drink entries, estimates, settings, notification preferences, and any optional drink photos locally on your device. If you choose to sign in, the cloud-sync data described below may also be stored with your account. Drink photos are never uploaded by Half-Life.

## Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. After you choose to connect Apple Health, Half-Life may read heart-rate and sleep data to personalize a caffeine-clearance estimate. Raw Apple Health samples are processed on-device and are not persisted or uploaded by Half-Life. If you explicitly apply a suggested sleep or wake time, that selected value becomes a regular profile setting and may sync when you sign in; the underlying Apple Health samples and source records do not sync.

## Optional cloud sync

If you sign in, Half-Life uses Supabase to store your account email, drink entries, notes, and safe profile settings such as display name, usual caffeine dose, caffeine sensitivity, selected sleep and wake times, and late deadline. Notification preferences and private drink photos remain device-local. Cloud data is used solely to provide authentication and sync. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used for cross-app tracking.

Supabase processes cloud data on Half-Life's behalf. Data is retained while your cloud account remains active or while required to complete a pending deletion operation.

## Your choices

You can edit or remove individual records in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. If you created a cloud account, use **You → Data → Delete account** to permanently delete the account and its synced records. You may also request help through the support channel below.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion support, use the monitored contact published with the Half-Life App Store or TestFlight listing, or open a support request at https://github.com/leorao-enming/half-life-app/issues.
